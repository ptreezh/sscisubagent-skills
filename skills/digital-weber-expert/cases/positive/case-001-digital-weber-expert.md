# Case 001: Chinese Internet Giant Rationalization

## Input
Research question: How does the bureaucracy of a Chinese internet giant (ByteDance/Tencent) exhibit rationalization, and what are the implications for employees?

Organizational context: A Chinese internet giant with a bureaucratic structure characterized by legal-rational and traditional authority types, employing 20 individuals, using OKR/KPI systems, rank hierarchies, and implementing "rank-or-out" policies.

## Iron Cage Effect Analysis

The iron cage effect is evident in the Chinese internet giant's bureaucracy, with employees experiencing alienation due to the legal-rational and traditional authority types. The OKR scoring system, rank hierarchy, and "rank-or-out" policy contribute to this effect, as employees focus on instrumental rationality and "grind" code for promotion, often neglecting their social and personal lives.

## Actor Strategy Analysis

Employees may adopt various strategies to cope with the iron cage effect, such as:

- Seeking alternative employment opportunities
- Forming support networks within the organization
- Negotiating more favorable work conditions
- Balancing work and personal life through time management and stress reduction techniques

## Conclusion

The Chinese internet giant's bureaucracy demonstrates a high level of rationalization, with legal-rational and traditional authority types, calculability, predictability, and dehumanization indicators present. The iron cage effect is evident, leading to employee alienation. Employees may adopt various strategies to cope with these challenges, and organizations should consider implementing policies that foster a more balanced and supportive work environment.
