Research question: How do Weibo hot-searches impact social solidarity mechanisms?

## Social Fact Type Identification

In this case, the social facts are non-material, consisting of collective emotions and public pressure. Collective emotions refer to the shared emotional responses of internet users to a particular hot-search topic, while public pressure refers to the social pressure exerted on individuals or groups through the hot-search mechanism.

## Solidarity Mechanism Evaluation

The solidarity mechanism transformation in this case is from traditional mechanical solidarity to modern organic solidarity. The traditional mechanical solidarity is evident in the shared values and beliefs of the collective conscience, while the modern organic solidarity is reflected in the interdependence and cooperation among internet users in the virtual community. The hot-search mechanism ruptures the traditional solidarity by creating a simulated environment that deviates from the real social consensus, leading to the reorganization of solidarity around new forms of interdependence and cooperation.

## Anomie Risk Score

The anomie risk in this case is moderate, with the potential for opinion reversal and online violence. Opinion reversal refers to the phenomenon where the collective conscience is manipulated or distorted by external forces, leading to a breakdown of shared values and beliefs. Online violence refers to the aggressive or harmful behavior exhibited by internet users towards others, which can undermine social solidarity and lead to anomie.

## Collective Conscience Analysis

The collective conscience in this case is the shared values and beliefs of Chinese internet users on Weibo. The hot-search mechanism creates a simulated environment that deviates from the real social consensus, leading to a rupture of traditional mechanical solidarity. However, the collective conscience remains present in the form of public pressure and collective emotions, which exert a strong influence on individual behavior and shape the reorganization of solidarity around new forms of interdependence and cooperation.

In conclusion, the Weibo hot-search mechanism has a significant impact on social solidarity mechanisms, transforming traditional mechanical solidarity into modern organic solidarity through the rupture and reorganization of the collective conscience. While the anomie risk is moderate, the collective conscience remains a powerful force in shaping individual behavior and social solidarity in the virtual community.
